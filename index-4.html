Who We Are

Education with purpose and values

Established in 2018 under the administration of Haji Jamaluddin Sahib, Tameer-e-Seerat Public High School serves students from Nursery to Class X in Gaje Jo Par, Tharparkar.

Our aim is to support academic learning, confidence, discipline and character development. The school has completed four student batches, with its first matriculation batch graduating in 2023.

  • Classes from Nursery to Class X
  • Sindh Textbook Board (STB) syllabus
  • Regular General Tests and academic assessment
  • Teaching faculty and learning support
  • School activities and student participation
